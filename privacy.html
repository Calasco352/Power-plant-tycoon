Power Plant Tycoon Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Overview

Calasco Interactive respects your privacy. This policy explains how information may be handled when you use Power Plant Tycoon.

Game Progress

Power Plant Tycoon may store gameplay progress and settings locally on your device. Deleting the app may remove locally stored progress unless a separate backup or platform feature preserves it.

In-App Purchases

In-app purchases are processed through Apple’s App Store and StoreKit. Calasco Interactive does not receive your full payment card information. Apple may process purchase and transaction information under Apple’s own privacy practices.

Support Communications

If you contact us for support, we may receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, device details, screenshots, screen recordings, or a description of the issue. We use this information only to respond to your request and troubleshoot the problem.

Advertising and Third-Party Services

If a released version of Power Plant Tycoon includes optional or interstitial advertising, the advertising provider may process device or advertising-related information according to that provider’s privacy policy and Apple’s platform rules. Any App Store privacy disclosures for the released version should reflect the services actually enabled in that build.

Sale of Personal Information

Calasco Interactive does not sell personal information collected through direct support communications.

Children

Power Plant Tycoon is a general-audience game. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through support, contact us so we can review and address the request.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the app, its services, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be sent to calascointeractive.dev@gmail.com.
