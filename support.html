Contact Support

Email Support

Support email: calascointeractive.dev@gmail.com

Common Help

  • In-app purchase not showing: Open the in-game Store and use Restore Purchases for permanent purchases.
  • Progress issue: Do not delete the app before contacting support, because game progress may be stored locally on your device.
  • Gameplay bug: A screenshot or short screen recording is very helpful.
  • Billing/refund questions: Purchases are processed by Apple. Apple manages payment details and refund requests.
