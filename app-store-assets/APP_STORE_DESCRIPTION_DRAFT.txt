PROMOTIONAL TEXT
Start with a small generating station and grow it into a global energy empire. Build advanced power assets, master the grid, complete contracts, expand across regions, and push your company into the trillion-dollar era.

DESCRIPTION
Take control of the grid in Power Plant Tycoon, an industrial idle management game where every kilowatt can become the foundation of an energy empire.

Generate and sell electricity, commission new plants, manage maintenance and reliability, respond to market conditions, complete utility contracts, research advanced technology, and expand your transmission network across increasingly ambitious regions.

Your company begins at Riverbend Station, but the grid does not end there. Develop diesel, steam, gas, solar and nuclear generation before advancing into offshore wind, pumped hydro, small modular reactors, geothermal power, fusion and orbital energy infrastructure.

FEATURES
• Build and upgrade an expanding generation fleet
• Grow from kW-scale operations into MW, GW, TW and beyond
• Trade electricity against a changing energy market
• Manage reliability, maintenance, staffing and operating policy
• Complete contracts, missions, achievements and mega projects
• Expand from a local utility into a planetary-scale energy authority
• Prestige for permanent bonuses and continue through repeatable Empire Levels
• Earn offline production while you are away
• Optional rewarded ads for temporary bonuses
• Optional in-app purchases including permanent quality-of-life upgrades
